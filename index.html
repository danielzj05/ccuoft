DESIGN. CAST. RACE.

The Concrete Canoe Team is a student-run, multi-disciplinary design team that endeavours to build the best concrete canoe we possibly can! While "Concrete" may give us a slight civil engineering bias, we are proud to have talent from disciplines across both engineering and artsci contributing to our team. We maintain our own hull structural design testing software, we dedicate a team to sustainability, we must come up with an appealing aesthetic and theme, and of course, we must have a strong paddling crew to race the canoe!

The zenith of every year's design cycle is the annual Canadian National Concrete Canoe Competition (CNCCC), hosted by the Canadian Society of Civil Engineers in parallel with the Steel Bridge and Water Treatment Competition. The 2027 CNCCC will be held at the University of Waterloo between the 12th and 16th of May.
