Our Sponsors

We are proud to partner with a diverse range of organizations that support our mission to provide hands-on engineering experience to students at the University of Toronto. Our sponsors play a crucial role in our success, providing resources, expertise, and opportunities that help us design, build, and compete with the best teams in the region.

Gold Sponsors

Soprema

For more than 100 years, SOPREMA has been using its expertise to develop a variety of high-end products that measure up to the requirements of the construction field.

Centralized Process for Student Initiative Funding (CPSIF)

The Centralized Process for Student Initiative Funding (CPSIF) allows student groups to apply to various funding sources from within the Faculty of Applied Science & Engineering in a single application. Departments and affiliates of this program will all receive the same application and budget, and will use this information to review student group funding requests.

SpiderLath™ delivers unparalleled performance with a lightweight design, faster installation, and unmatched durability. Discover a fiberglass lath system that saves time and money without compromising on quality.
